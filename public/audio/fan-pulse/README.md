# Fan Pulse Audio Sources

These MP3 files are Mixkit sound effects downloaded for local use in the fan pulse UI.
Mixkit states its sound effects are free to download and ready to use under the Mixkit License.

- `fire.mp3`: Short fire whoosh, https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/fire/
- `blast-hit.mp3`: Wood hard hit, https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/hit/
- `cheer.mp3`: Stadium crowd light applause, https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/cheer/
- `celebrate.mp3`: Huge crowd cheering victory, https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/crowd/
- `shock.mp3`: Female astonished gasp, https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/gasp/

License page: https://mixkit.co/license/
